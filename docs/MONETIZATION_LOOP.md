# Monetization Loop

The agent should optimize one measurable loop instead of producing content without feedback:

1. Discover or verify an affiliate program.
2. Score the opportunity by payout, buyer intent, attribution window, audience fit and competition.
3. Select a campaign blueprint.
4. Create commercial content with a clear affiliate disclosure.
5. Send every call-to-action through the workspace tracking redirect.
6. Record human clicks, conversions, commission and gross sales.
7. Calculate conversion rate and EPC.
8. Feed performance back into future prioritization.

## Initial priority

### 1. Hostinger Horizons

Use commercial-investigation, comparison and tutorial content aimed at founders, freelancers and small businesses evaluating AI app builders. Do not represent the maximum published commission as guaranteed. Use an approved affiliate account and the affiliate link issued to that account.

### 2. Semrush

Use SEO and AI-visibility tutorials, reviews and comparisons. Track trials separately from purchases because they can have separate commission events.

## Launch gate

A campaign must remain in `draft` status until an approved affiliate link has been inserted. Activate tracking only after the affiliate account is approved and the destination URL is verified.

## Metrics

The first decision metrics are human clicks, approved conversions, conversion rate, commission revenue and EPC. Page views alone are not treated as a success metric.
