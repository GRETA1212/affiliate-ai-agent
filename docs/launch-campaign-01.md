## First demonstration workflow

Build a simple "Lead Follow-up Agent":

1. Capture a prospect/request
2. Classify the request
3. Generate a follow-up task
4. Draft a response
5. Place the task in a simple pipeline

The goal is to create evidence for the content instead of publishing generic AI-generated claims.

## Conversion rules

- Never claim guaranteed revenue or productivity gains.
- Always disclose affiliate relationships near the CTA.
- Do not self-refer.
- Route public affiliate clicks through `/go/taskade` so attribution is captured before redirecting to Taskade.
- Measure clicks, paid conversions, EPC, and RPM before scaling.

## Kill / scale thresholds

Do not judge the offer from a handful of impressions.

Initial evidence gate:

- 1,000+ relevant landing-page impressions OR 100+ affiliate-intent visits
- at least 30 tracked outbound clicks before making a first conversion judgment

Scale only when the campaign has positive commission revenue and its EPC/RPM is stronger than competing campaigns in our own data.
