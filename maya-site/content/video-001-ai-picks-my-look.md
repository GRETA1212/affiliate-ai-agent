# Maya.exe Video 001 — AI Picks My Look

## Goal
Create Maya's first short-form video as a creator experiment, not an ad. The first 2/3 of the video should earn attention through curiosity and personality. Products appear naturally only where they are actually used, and any affiliate relationship is disclosed.

## Format
- 9:16 vertical master
- 28–35 seconds
- Export once, then adapt captions/title for TikTok and YouTube Shorts
- Visual style: cozy vanity, warm daylight, handheld phone-camera feel, small imperfections, natural pauses, subtle facial expressions
- Maya must remain clearly disclosed as a virtual/AI creator in profile/bio and platform AI-content labels when required

## Hook
On-screen text: **I gave AI one sentence and let it choose my entire makeup look.**

Maya: "Okay... I gave AI one sentence: make me look expensive, but like I only had ten minutes. This is what it picked."

## Shot plan
### 0:00–0:03 — Cold open
Close handheld crop at the vanity. Maya looks at the phone, then camera. Do not begin with a product.

Dialogue: "Okay... this could be either really good or really embarrassing."

On-screen: `AI picked my whole look`

### 0:03–0:07 — Show the prompt
Quick over-shoulder phone shot. Prompt card is visible for about 1.5 seconds.

Prompt: `Create a polished 10-minute makeup look. Keep it soft, wearable and slightly glossy.`

Maya: "I gave it one rule: ten minutes."

### 0:07–0:13 — First product / complexion
Cut to Maya applying the first VERIFIED product actually used in the rendered scene.

Maya: "It wanted the base really light, which I actually agree with."

Small lower-third only after the product appears:
`Product used · affiliate link if applicable`

### 0:13–0:19 — Eyes / brush
Faster two-shot sequence. Include a tiny human moment: Maya misses the brush holder, laughs lightly, or checks the mirror twice. Do not overdo it.

Maya: "Then this tiny wing. AI has more confidence in my eyeliner than I do."

### 0:19–0:24 — Lip / reveal setup
Close-up of the exact lip product used.

Maya: "And it chose a gloss I would honestly have ignored."

### 0:24–0:29 — Reveal
Camera backs up slightly. Maya checks both sides of her face in the mirror.

Maya: "Wait... okay. I hate that it was right."

On-screen: `Would you wear this?`

### 0:29–0:34 — CTA
Keep CTA conversational, not salesy.

Maya: "I'll put the exact products from this look in my shop page. Next, I'm letting AI choose the budget."

On-screen CTA:
`Exact products → Maya.exe/shop`

If native TikTok Shop product tagging is available and the exact product listing is verified, tag that listing instead of using vague product claims.

## Affiliate/product rule
Do not publish "exact products" or tag a product until ALL of the following are true:
1. The listing URL is verified.
2. The product shown in the video matches the listing.
3. Current price is checked on publish day if price is mentioned.
4. The affiliate relationship is disclosed.
5. Any performance/beauty claim used in copy has evidence or is framed as Maya's subjective reaction rather than fact.

If a real listing is not ready, use this CTA instead:
`Full experiment + product list coming to Maya.exe`

- Affiliate/sponsored disclosures must be visible wherever the platform or local advertising rules require them.

## Product slots for Video 001
Use only real products after verification. Preferred order:
1. complexion/base product
2. eye product or brush/tool
3. lip product

Do not force an LED mask, smart mirror or other unrelated gadget into this episode just because it has a higher commission. The product must belong naturally in the story.

## Success metrics
Primary: 3-second hold rate, average percentage viewed, rewatches.
Secondary: profile visits, shop-page clicks, product clicks.
Revenue: commission per 1,000 video views.
