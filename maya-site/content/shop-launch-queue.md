## Activation checklist for every product

- Exact product listing URL captured from the approved affiliate/creator account.
- Product name matches the live listing.
- Current price checked on the same day the link is activated.
- Any discount is real and current; no invented countdowns.
- Seller/store identity recorded.
- Shipping market confirmed for the intended audience.
- Return/refund terms checked.
- Product claims separated from Maya.exe editorial claims.
- Health, skin, light-therapy, or safety claims require reliable supporting sources before being repeated.
- A content angle exists: test, comparison, tutorial, problem/solution, or buying checklist.
- Affiliate/sponsored relationship disclosed near the CTA.
- Outbound link uses `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer"`.
- Click source/content label chosen for analytics.

## Status definitions

**Researching** — no recommendation; claims, listing, market and use case are being checked.

**Testing queue** — a real test or comparison method is defined but has not been completed.

**Reviewed** — a documented evaluation exists and the published copy accurately reflects it.

**Live affiliate pick** — reviewed/verified listing plus an active affiliate destination. This is the only state that should show a live Shop CTA.

## Initial monetization priority

Start with creator gear and simple makeup tools because claims are easier to verify. Treat LED devices, skin scanners and other health-adjacent products more cautiously and do not publish efficacy claims from seller copy alone.
