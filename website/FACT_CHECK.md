# Commercial content fact-check register

Last reviewed: **11 August 2026**

This register records the official sources used for named commercial buyer guides. It exists so future updates can re-check claims before publishing or refreshing articles.

## Lovable

Official sources:

- Pricing / credits / code ownership: https://lovable.dev/pricing
- Discoverability announcement: https://lovable.dev/blog/building-is-just-the-beginning-introducing-discoverability
- Affiliate program: https://lovable.dev/partners/affiliates

Claims currently used:

- Lovable describes itself as an AI software engineer for building websites/web apps through chat.
- Free plan: 5 daily build credits, up to 30/month; 20 monthly Cloud credits; 4 credits for AI features in user apps.
- Paid credits can be used across building, Cloud and AI features.
- Workspaces support unlimited members and are not priced per seat.
- Lovable states customers own generated code/customer data/AI output, subject to third-party rights.
- Discoverability capabilities and Semrush integration were announced in May 2026.

Do not hard-code paid-plan dollar prices until an official source exposes stable current values in the page content used by the checker.

- Affiliate program: https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates

Claims currently used:

- ElevenLabs describes ElevenCreative, ElevenAgents and ElevenAPI as its three product platforms.
- ElevenCreative is described as supporting creation/editing across 70+ languages.
- Public plan names: Free, Starter, Creator, Pro, Scale and Business, plus Enterprise.
- Subscription and Pay As You Go billing options are documented.
- Affiliate program: 22% of eligible payments for first 12 months on Starter/Creator/Pro/Scale referrals; 11% on Business; 90-day cookie; PartnerStack; commissions paid after referred license has remained active over 90 days.

- Affiliate terms page last updated 10 June 2026 when checked.

Affiliate economics must never be used as evidence of product quality.

## Editorial controls

- Named commercial pages must display a visible fact-check date.
- Use official vendor sources for product/pricing/program claims whenever available.
- Do not claim hands-on testing unless it was actually performed and documented.
- Do not fabricate affiliate approval, tracking links, earnings, testimonials, reviews or conversions.
- Affiliate links must be clearly disclosed and should use `rel="sponsored"` once implemented.
- Product rankings must be based on user/job fit, not affiliate payout.
- Re-check all price, plan and affiliate-program claims before changing `SITE_URL` from the placeholder and before a major article refresh.
