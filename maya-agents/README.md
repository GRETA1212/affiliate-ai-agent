# Maya Agent OS

Maya Agent OS is the operating layer for turning Maya.exe into a measurable beauty-tech media and commerce business.

## Objective

Build a repeatable system that moves from verified demand to content to traffic to monetization, without fake metrics, fake product tests, fake reviews, fake discounts, fake identities, or unverified affiliate claims.

Primary business loop:

`Trend research -> Product research -> Content brief -> Script/production -> Publish -> Traffic -> Website -> Affiliate/owned offer -> Analytics -> Scale/kill decision`

## Agents

1. **Orchestrator** — owns the daily queue and enforces gates.
2. **Trend Scout** — finds current Italy beauty/beauty-tech demand from primary/current sources.
3. **Product Scout** — scores products by demand, visual demo potential, commission economics, seller quality, Maya fit, and risk.
4. **Content Strategist** — turns verified trends/products into testable content angles.
5. **Script Producer** — writes TikTok/Shorts scripts, shot lists, hooks, captions, disclosures, and CTA variants.
6. **Publishing Planner** — maintains the posting calendar, channel adaptation, UTM/content IDs, and reuse rules.
7. **Commerce & Compliance** — verifies affiliate destinations, prices, claims, disclosures, platform eligibility, and KYC/payee assumptions before monetized links go live.
8. **Funnel Agent** — creates/updates Maya website landing-page briefs and maps every video to one destination.
9. **Analytics Agent** — records views, retention, profile/site clicks, affiliate clicks, orders, revenue, and revenue per 1,000 views.
10. **Growth Optimizer** — scales top performers, kills weak formats, and proposes the next experiments from real data.
11. **B2B Sales Agent** — turns Maya proof into qualified beauty-business outreach for the higher-value service offer.

## Hard gates

- No product is called "tested" unless a real documented test exists.
- No exact product is presented as used by Maya unless the creative and destination match the verified product.
- No price, discount, commission, sales count, rating, or eligibility rule is published from memory.
- Current program rules must be checked from official/platform sources before action.
- AI/virtual-creator disclosure stays visible where required.
- No fake geography, age, KYC, payee, bank, or tax details.
- No paid traffic, product purchases, software subscriptions, or ad spend without a written expected-outcome test and user approval.
- No bot traffic, fake followers, engagement manipulation, fake reviews, or deceptive endorsements.

## Daily operating sequence

1. Trend Scout returns 5 evidence-backed opportunities.
2. Product Scout scores any monetizable candidates.
3. Orchestrator selects at most 2 content experiments for the day.
4. Content Strategist + Script Producer create two hook variants per experiment.
5. Commerce & Compliance clears the claims/links/disclosures.
6. Publishing Planner assigns `V###` IDs and channel variants.
7. Funnel Agent confirms the destination page and tracking parameters.
8. After publishing, Analytics Agent records results.
9. Growth Optimizer uses results to scale, revise, or kill the format.

## Decision metrics

Minimum dataset per video:

- content_id
- platform
- publish_time
- views
- average_watch_time or retention where available
- likes/comments/shares/saves
- profile_visits
- site_sessions
- affiliate_clicks
- orders/leads
- gross_revenue
- commission_revenue
- owned_product_revenue
- revenue_per_1000_views

Never invent missing values. Store them as blank/unknown until measured.

## 30-day goal

The first month is a validation sprint, not a guaranteed €10k month. The system's job is to discover whether Maya can produce a repeatable path from views to clicks to sales/leads, then estimate the €10k requirement from actual conversion and commission data.

See `agents.yaml` for agent contracts and `runbooks/30-day-plan.md` for the execution plan.
