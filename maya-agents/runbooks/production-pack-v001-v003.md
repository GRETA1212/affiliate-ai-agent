# Maya.exe production pack — V001 to V003

Purpose: get the first three Maya experiments filmed/assembled and published with the smallest affordable production stack. These are pre-commerce tests: no exact product is claimed as used, tested, priced, discounted or recommended unless separate real evidence exists.

## Shared production rules

- 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 export when practical.
- Aim for 20–30 seconds.
- Maya disclosure visible: `Virtual AI creator`.
- Burn subtitles.
- Use natural phone/UGC pacing, not glossy commercial pacing.
- Do not show branded packaging unless it is a real verified product actually involved in the video.
- Do not say `I tested`, `I used`, `this product`, or quote a price unless documented evidence supports it.
- TikTok and YouTube Shorts use the same master creative with platform-specific captions/CTA.
- Content IDs: V001, V002, V003.

# V001 — AI picked my 10-minute makeup look

## Goal
Establish Maya's core repeatable format and measure retention, follows, profile visits and site interest.

## Duration target
24–28 seconds.

## Hook A
`Okay… I gave AI one sentence and told it to choose my whole makeup look.`

## Hook B
`I told AI: make me look expensive, but I only have ten minutes.`

Use Hook B first unless production makes Hook A materially stronger. Do not post both simultaneously; test the second as a later controlled variation.

## Shot-by-shot

### 0.0–2.5s — Hook
Maya chest-up at the vanity, direct eye contact. Slight natural camera movement.

On-screen text: `AI PICKED MY MAKEUP 😳`

Spoken: `I told AI: make me look expensive, but I only have ten minutes.`

### 2.5–5.5s — Prompt proof
Show phone/prompt card clearly enough to read:

`Make me look expensive, but like I only had ten minutes.`

Spoken: `That was literally the whole prompt.`

### 5.5–14.5s — Fast build
Three or four close-up beats:
- soft brown eye/liner direction
- peach blush direction
- nude-pink glossy lip direction
- optional final blending beat

Do not show fake branded products.

Spoken: `It went soft brown on the eyes, peach on the cheeks, and glossy nude-pink on the lips.`

### 14.5–21.5s — Reveal
Hold the final look long enough to judge it.

Spoken: `And… wait. Okay. I hate that it was kind of right.`

### 21.5–26.0s — CTA
On-screen: `Full look → maya-exe.vercel.app`

Spoken: `The full look direction is on the Maya site.`

Small disclosure remains visible: `Virtual AI creator`.

## TikTok caption
`One sentence. One full makeup direction. AI did better than I expected 😳 Which part should it choose next — blush or lips? #AIMakeup #BeautyTech #VirtualCreator #MakeupTok`

## YouTube Shorts title
`AI Picked My Entire 10-Minute Makeup Look 😳`

## YouTube description
`Maya.exe is a virtual AI creator. This video shows an AI-designed makeup direction, not a claim that specific physical products were tested or used.`

## Destination
`https://maya-exe.vercel.app/shop-the-look/ai-picked-makeup/`

Do not use this CTA until the live deployment returns 200. If it is still unavailable, use the Maya homepage/profile CTA instead.

## Primary metrics
- completion/retention
- followers gained per 1,000 views
- profile visits per 1,000 views
- site sessions per 1,000 views once destination is live

# V002 — 3 things AI got wrong

## Goal
Build trust and test whether critique performs better than pure transformation.

## Duration target
22–27 seconds.

## Hook A
`AI picked the look. Here are the three things it got wrong.`

## Hook B
`This is why I would never let AI choose makeup without checking it.`

## Shot-by-shot

### 0.0–2.5s — Hook
Show final V001-style look, then quick skeptical reaction.

On-screen: `3 THINGS AI GOT WRONG`

### 2.5–8.0s — Problem 1
`First: it tried to make the eye definition stronger than a real ten-minute routine needs.`

Visual: compare heavier vs softer eye direction. Keep this an aesthetic/time critique, not a product-performance claim.

### 8.0–13.5s — Problem 2
`Second: the first blush direction fought the lip instead of helping it.`

Visual: simple color-direction comparison.

### 13.5–19.0s — Problem 3
`Third: it designed the perfect-looking version, not the fastest practical version.`

Visual: too-many-steps graphic / simplified version.

### 19.0–24.0s — CTA
`Should I make AI fix all three in one prompt?`

On-screen: `PART 2?`

## TikTok caption
`AI can make a pretty concept and still miss the practical part. Should Maya make it fix all 3? #AIMakeup #BeautyExperiment #VirtualCreator`

## YouTube Shorts title
`3 Things AI Got Wrong About This Makeup Look`

## Primary metrics
- comment rate
- completion rate
- follows per 1,000 views

## No-claim rule
The three mistakes must correspond to what is visibly present in the actual creative. Do not invent a failure that viewers cannot see.

# V003 — Peach blush vs pink blush

## Goal
Test a very simple participatory A/B format that can become a repeatable series.

## Duration target
18–23 seconds.

## Hook A
`Same face. Two blush directions. Which one wins?`

## Hook B
`AI chose peach. I think pink might beat it.`

## Shot-by-shot

### 0.0–2.0s — Hook
Immediate split screen or rapid A/B flashes.

On-screen: `PEACH vs PINK`

### 2.0–7.5s — Peach
Show peach direction for enough time to judge.

Spoken: `AI picked peach because it keeps the whole look warmer.`

### 7.5–13.0s — Pink
Show pink direction under the same framing/lighting.

Spoken: `But pink changes the whole mood.`

### 13.0–18.5s — A/B comparison
Split screen, same size and exposure.

Spoken: `No explanation. Which one actually wins?`

### 18.5–21.5s — CTA
On-screen: `COMMENT: PEACH or PINK`

Spoken: `Peach or pink? The winner becomes the next look.`

## TikTok caption
`Same face, one variable. PEACH or PINK? Winner becomes Maya's next look. #Blush #MakeupTok #AIMakeup #VirtualCreator`

## YouTube Shorts title
`Peach Blush vs Pink Blush — Which One Wins?`

## Primary metrics
- comments per 1,000 views
- completion rate
- follows per 1,000 views
