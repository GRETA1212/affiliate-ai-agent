# V011 — Beauty-tech electronics: worth it or gimmick?

## Why this is V011, not V001

V001 is already reserved for `AI picked my 10-minute makeup look`. Reusing V001 for a different experiment would contaminate analytics. This beauty-tech electronics test therefore starts as V011.

## Objective

Test whether Maya's audience responds to higher-intent beauty-tech education before any exact TikTok Shop product is approved.

This is a category-level content test only. No specific SKU, seller, price, creator commission, sales velocity, or product-performance claim is approved yet.

## Format

- Vertical 9:16
- Target length: 22–28 seconds
- Maya may appear as a realistic synthetic host.
- Keep a small persistent on-screen badge: `Virtual AI creator`.
- Turn on TikTok's `AI-generated content` setting when posting realistic AIGC.
- Do not show a branded product unless the exact product/listing has been verified.

## Hook A

`Beauty tech looks magical until you ask three boring questions.`

## Hook B

`Before I recommend any beauty gadget, it has to survive these three checks.`

## Commercial-disclosure rule

For this pre-commerce version, do not add an affiliate/product link because none is approved.

If a later version promotes a specific third-party product in exchange for commission or another incentive:

1. Turn on TikTok's commercial-content disclosure setting.
2. Select the appropriate third-party/branded-content disclosure.
3. Keep the AI-generated-content disclosure separately; one disclosure does not replace the other.
4. Add a clear affiliate disclosure near the commercial CTA, e.g. `Link affiliato — posso ricevere una commissione`.
5. Use only the exact verified listing and current product facts.

If the post instead promotes Maya's own paid product/service, use TikTok's `Your brand` / promotional-content disclosure path.

## Evidence required before a shoppable V011 variant

Product Scout must supply:

- exact product name
- exact seller
- exact TikTok Shop listing URL
- current price
- Maya creator affiliate rate
- commission EUR/order
- available Creator Center demand/sales signals
- sample availability
- seller-quality evidence
- return/warranty information where available
- applicable claims/safety/compliance evidence
- evidence date

Unknown values remain `UNKNOWN`; they are never estimated and presented as facts.

## Do not claim

- `I tested it` unless a real documented test occurred.
- that Maya physically used a device.
- a specific safety, medical, skin, hair, or efficacy outcome without adequate evidence.
- a creator commission based on TikTok's seller platform fee.
- a current price, discount, sales count, rating, or best-seller status without exact current evidence.
- that an EU-stocked product is compliant merely because it ships from the EU.
