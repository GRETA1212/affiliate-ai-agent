# Maya.exe launch queue — V001 to V010

Purpose: publish the first controlled Maya experiments, collect comparable traffic data, and discover which format deserves more production. Nothing in this queue assumes a product has been tested, purchased, received, or approved for affiliate promotion.

## Global production rules

- Format: 9:16 vertical.
- First-test length: 20–35 seconds unless the edit is clearly stronger shorter.
- Keep a visible disclosure such as `Virtual AI creator` when Maya appears as a realistic synthetic person.
- Do not show invented branded products, prices, discounts, commissions, reviews, sales counts, or test results.
- If real product footage is not available, describe a look/category rather than claiming Maya used a specific product.
- Every master creative gets one content ID and two hook variants. Change only one major variable between variants.
- Primary pre-commerce success signals: retention/watch time, follows per 1,000 views, profile visits per 1,000 views, and site sessions per 1,000 views.

## V001 — AI picked my 10-minute makeup look

**Goal:** establish the core Maya format.

**Hook A:** `I gave AI one sentence and told it to choose my whole makeup look.`

**Hook B:** `I told AI: make me look expensive, but I only have ten minutes.`

**Spoken structure:**
1. `Okay… I gave AI one sentence and told it to choose my whole makeup look.`
2. Show the prompt: `Make me look expensive, but like I only had ten minutes.`
3. Fast reveal/build sequence: warm skin, brown liner/lashes, peach blush, nude-pink glossy lip.
4. Reaction: `And… wait. Okay. I hate that it was kind of right.`
5. CTA: `The full look is on the Maya site.`

**On-screen text:** `AI PICKED MY MAKEUP 😳`

**Destination:** `/shop-the-look/ai-picked-makeup/` once live; until then use the Maya homepage.

**Tracking:** `utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=v001`

**No-claim rule:** do not say a specific product was used unless real documented footage supports it.

## V002 — 3 things AI got wrong

**Goal:** trust-building; show that Maya does not blindly praise AI.

**Hook A:** `AI picked the look. Here are the three things it got wrong.`

**Hook B:** `This is why I would never let AI choose makeup without checking it.`

**Story:** show three concrete aesthetic problems with the generated recommendation, such as too much contrast, impractical timing, or a shade direction that does not fit the final look. Keep the critique about the look itself, not medical/skin claims.

**CTA:** `Should I make AI fix it?`

**Success signal:** comments + follows per 1,000 views.

**Tracking:** `utm_content=v002`

# Platform adaptation

## TikTok

- Primary CTA before Shop eligibility: follow/profile/site where a legitimate clickable route is available.
- After Shop eligibility: use exact approved product cards only for products/listings actually verified.
- Keep commercial and AI disclosures visible when required.

## YouTube Shorts

- Use the same master creative where useful.
- Do not rely on a Shorts-description URL as the main funnel.
- Use channel profile links and/or a related long-form video when configured.
- Title should describe the experiment, not overpromise the result.

# Daily decision rule

After each comparable measurement window:

1. Analytics Agent records actual values; unknown stays blank/unknown, never `0` by assumption.
2. Growth Optimizer ranks by site sessions per 1,000 views + retention until revenue data exists.
3. A top-quartile concept receives three controlled follow-ups.
4. A bottom-quartile concept pauses unless the Orchestrator documents a strategic reason.
5. Once revenue exists, revenue per 1,000 views becomes the main commercial ranking.
